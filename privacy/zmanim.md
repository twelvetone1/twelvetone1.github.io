# Privacy Policy for Zmanim

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Zmanim is a Wear OS app and tile developed by Steven Kahn ("I").

## What the app accesses

- **Approximate location (optional).** If you tap "Use my location", the app reads
  your watch's approximate location once and saves it on the watch. It is used only
  on the watch to calculate sunrise, sunset, candle-lighting and Havdalah times. You
  can choose a city from a built-in list instead and never grant location access.

## What the app collects or shares

- **Nothing.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting,
  and it makes no network connections. No data leaves your watch.

## Data retention and deletion

- Your settings (location or city, Israel/diaspora, candle-lighting minutes) are
  stored only on your watch. They are not included in device backups or transfers
  to a new watch. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Children

- The app is not directed at children under 13.
