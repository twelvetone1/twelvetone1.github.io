# Privacy Policy for HA Relay

Last updated: November 20, 2026

HA Relay is an Android phone app and Wear OS app and tile, developed by Steven Kahn ("I"). It lets
the watch control lights in your own Home Assistant installation through your phone.

## What the app accesses

- **Your Home Assistant URLs and access token.** You enter these in the phone app. They are stored
  only on your phone and are never sent to the watch.
- **Your Home Assistant server.** The phone app connects only to the URLs you enter. It reads and
  sets the light modes the watch asks for.
- **Network state.** The phone checks whether it is on Wi-Fi, to decide which of your URLs to try
  first.

## What the app collects or shares

- **Nothing is sent to me or to anyone else.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or
  crash reporting. Its only network connections are to your own Home Assistant server.
- **Diagnostics log.** Each app keeps a small log on its own device: room names, light modes,
  Home Assistant entity IDs and timings, but no URLs, token or server responses. It leaves your
  devices only if you tap **Share log** and choose where to send it. **Send log to phone** copies
  the watch's log to your phone over the watch–phone connection.

## Data retention and deletion

- Your settings and the logs stay on your phone and watch. They are not included in device
  backups. **Clear log** deletes the logs, and uninstalling the app deletes everything.

## Children

- The app is not directed at children under 13.
